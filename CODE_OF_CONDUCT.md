# Code of Conduct

Glyph contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and productive for people of every background and experience level.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, welcome constructive questions, and give specific technical feedback. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing another person's private material are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or, when confidentiality is important and no private contact is available, request a private contact without disclosing details publicly. Maintainers will limit disclosure to what is necessary to review and resolve the report. This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents Glyph elsewhere.
